US ecommerce sales topped $1.1 trillion in 2024 and continue to grow. Starting an online store has never been more accessible — or more competitive. The platform you build on determines your transaction fees, shipping options, payment methods, and how easily you can scale. Here are the best ecommerce platforms for US small businesses in 2026.

---

## What Matters When Choosing a US Ecommerce Platform

Before comparing platforms, understand the factors that affect your bottom line:

- **Transaction fees** — Some platforms charge 0.5–2% per sale on top of payment processing
- **Payment processing** — Who handles credit card charges and what do they cost?
- **US sales tax** — Does the platform calculate and collect the right tax for all 50 states?
- **Shipping integration** — Can it connect to UPS, FedEx, USPS, and print labels?
- **Inventory management** — Can it track stock across online and physical locations?
- **SEO** — Can Google find and rank your product pages?

---

## 1. Shopify — Best Overall for US Ecommerce

**Pricing:** $39/month (Basic) / $105/month (Shopify) / $399/month (Advanced)

Shopify powers more US small business online stores than any other platform — and for good reason. It handles everything: storefront design, inventory, payments, shipping, taxes, and marketing. Shopify Payments (their built-in processor) charges no transaction fees and accepts all US credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay installments.

**Best for:** Most US product-based businesses selling online

**Pros:**
- No transaction fees when using Shopify Payments
- Automatic US sales tax collection for all 50 states
- 8,000+ apps for extending functionality
- Point of sale system for selling in-person alongside online
- Abandoned cart recovery included on all plans
- Best shipping rates with UPS, USPS, and DHL discounts
- 24/7 customer support

**Cons:**
- Transaction fees (0.5–2%) if not using Shopify Payments
- Monthly cost higher than some competitors
- Advanced features require expensive apps

## 2. WooCommerce — Best for WordPress Users

**Pricing:** Free plugin / Hosting $5–25/month / Extensions vary

WooCommerce is a free plugin that turns any WordPress website into a full ecommerce store. For US businesses already running a WordPress site or blog, WooCommerce adds a complete online store without switching platforms. You own your data completely and pay no transaction fees beyond standard payment processor rates.

**Best for:** US businesses with existing WordPress sites, developers, content-heavy stores

**Pros:**
- Free to install — no monthly platform fee
- No transaction fees — only payment processor rates (typically 2.9% + $0.30)
- Total ownership of your store and data
- 59,000+ WordPress plugins extend functionality
- Best SEO capabilities of any ecommerce platform
- Scales from 1 product to 100,000+ products

**Cons:**
- Requires WordPress hosting and setup — not beginner-friendly
- Extensions and plugins add up in cost
- Security and updates are your responsibility
- Customer support requires paid extensions or community forums

---

## 3. BigCommerce — Best for High-Volume US Stores

**Pricing:** $39/month (Standard) / $105/month (Plus) / $399/month (Pro)

BigCommerce is built for US stores that are growing fast. It has no transaction fees on any plan, supports more product variants than Shopify, and includes features like customer groups, price lists, and B2B tools that Shopify charges extra for. For US wholesale businesses, brands selling to both consumers and retailers, or stores doing $500K+ per year, BigCommerce's built-in features are more cost-effective.

**Best for:** High-volume US stores, wholesale businesses, B2B sellers

**Pros:**
- Zero transaction fees on all plans — no hidden costs
- Supports more product variants than Shopify (600 vs 100)
- B2B features included — customer-specific pricing, purchase orders, net payment terms
- Multi-storefront — run multiple brands from one account
- Excellent SEO capabilities
- No limit on staff accounts

**Cons:**
- Annual sales limits — exceeding them bumps you to the next plan
- App ecosystem smaller than Shopify
- Interface less intuitive than Shopify

---

## 4. Wix eCommerce — Best for Simple US Online Stores

**Pricing:** $29/month (Core) / $36/month (Business) / $159/month (Business Elite)

For US small businesses that need a simple online store alongside a professional website — not a dedicated ecommerce operation — Wix Business plan combines website builder and store in one affordable subscription. Selling fewer than 100 products with straightforward fulfillment? Wix handles it without the complexity of Shopify or BigCommerce.

**Best for:** US service businesses selling a few products, local businesses adding online checkout

**Pros:**
- Best website design of any ecommerce builder
- Multichannel selling — list products on Instagram, Facebook, and Google Shopping
- Dropshipping integration with Modalyst
- Abandoned cart recovery on Business plan
- Bookings and appointments alongside product sales

**Cons:**
- Not suited for large catalogs or high-volume stores
- Transaction fees on lower plans
- Less powerful inventory management than dedicated ecommerce platforms

---

## 5. Etsy — Best for Handmade and Vintage US Sellers

**Pricing:** $0.20 per listing / 6.5% transaction fee / 3% + $0.25 payment processing

Etsy is not a traditional ecommerce platform — it is a marketplace where US buyers specifically search for handmade, vintage, and unique products. For US makers, crafters, and vintage sellers, Etsy's built-in audience of 90+ million buyers provides instant traffic that a standalone store takes months or years to build.

**Best for:** US handmade sellers, crafters, vintage resellers, artists, printable product creators

**Pros:**
- 90+ million active buyers already on the platform — instant audience
- No monthly fee — only pay when you sell
- Etsy Ads drive additional traffic to your listings
- Pattern by Etsy — standalone website using your Etsy inventory
- Global shipping tools built in
- Star Seller badge builds credibility with buyers

**Cons:**
- 6.5% transaction fee per sale — expensive at high volume
- Increasing competition makes standing out harder
- Platform algorithm changes can dramatically affect visibility
- You don't own the customer relationship — Etsy does

## US Sales Tax — Critical for All Online Sellers

Since the 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair Supreme Court decision, US online sellers must collect sales tax in states where they have economic nexus — typically $100,000 in sales or 200 transactions per year. All 45 states with sales tax have different rules.

Shopify, BigCommerce, and WooCommerce (with the WooCommerce Tax plugin) calculate and collect the correct state and local sales tax automatically. This is not optional — penalties for non-collection are significant.
