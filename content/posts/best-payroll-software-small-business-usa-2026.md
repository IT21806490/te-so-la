Running payroll manually is one of the most stressful things about owning a US small business. Get it wrong and you face IRS penalties, unhappy employees, and hours of back-and-forth with an accountant. The right payroll software handles federal and state tax filings automatically, pays your team on time, and keeps you compliant — all without needing a payroll specialist on staff.

Here are the best payroll software options for US small businesses in 2026, compared honestly on price, ease of use, and compliance features.

---

## What Good Payroll Software Does for US Businesses

Before comparing tools, here's what you should expect any modern payroll platform to handle:

- **Automatic tax calculations** — federal, state, and local withholding calculated correctly every pay run
- **Direct deposit** — employees paid on time without you writing checks
- **Tax filings** — W-2s, 1099s, 941s filed automatically with the IRS and state agencies
- **New hire reporting** — required by all 50 US states within days of hiring
- **Benefits deductions** — health insurance, 401(k), FSA deducted correctly
- **Workers' compensation** — pay-as-you-go insurance tied directly to payroll

If your current solution doesn't do all of this, you're carrying unnecessary risk.

## 1. Gusto — Best Overall for US Small Businesses

**Pricing:** $46/month + $6/employee (Simple) / $80/month + $12/employee (Plus)

Gusto is the most popular payroll software among US small businesses for good reason. It handles full-service payroll, automated tax filings in all 50 states, W-2 and 1099 filing, and new hire reporting — all automatically. The interface is genuinely the easiest in this category.

**Best for:** US small businesses with 1–100 employees, especially those without an HR department

**Pros:**
- Full-service payroll in all 50 states — taxes filed automatically
- W-2s and 1099s sent to employees and filed with the IRS automatically
- Built-in onboarding for new hires including I-9 and W-4 collection
- Integrates with QuickBooks, Xero, and most US accounting software
- Health insurance and 401(k) administration available
- Excellent mobile app for employees to view pay stubs

**Cons:**
- More expensive than basic alternatives
- Customer support can be slow during tax season
- Time tracking requires Plus plan

**Why we recommend it:** For most US small businesses, Gusto is the right choice. It eliminates the risk of payroll errors and IRS penalties, and the cost is justified by what you save in accounting fees alone.

## 2. QuickBooks Payroll — Best if You Already Use QuickBooks

**Pricing:** $50/month + $6/employee (Core) / $85/month + $9/employee (Premium) / $130/month + $11/employee (Elite)

If your US business already uses QuickBooks for accounting, their payroll add-on is a natural fit. Everything stays in one place — payroll expenses flow directly into your books without manual entry. The Elite plan includes a tax penalty protection guarantee, which is unique in this category.

**Best for:** US businesses already using QuickBooks Online for accounting

**Pros:**
- Seamless integration with QuickBooks accounting — no data entry
- Same-day direct deposit available
- Tax penalty protection on Elite plan (QuickBooks pays your IRS penalties)
- Strong reporting tied to your financial data
- HR support included on higher tiers

**Cons:**
- Expensive if you don't already use QuickBooks
- Support quality varies by tier
- Interface less intuitive than Gusto for payroll-only tasks

## 3. ADP Run — Best for Businesses Planning to Scale

**Pricing:** Custom quotes (typically $59–$160+/month + per-employee fees)

ADP is the largest payroll processor in the United States — they process payroll for 1 in 6 US workers. ADP Run is their small business product, and it gives you access to enterprise-grade compliance tools at small business pricing. If you plan to grow beyond 50 employees, ADP Run grows with you without switching platforms.

**Best for:** US businesses with 5–49 employees that expect rapid growth

**Pros:**
- Most robust compliance tools available — 24/7 compliance alerts
- Workers' compensation pay-as-you-go built in
- Access to ADP's extensive HR library and legal resources
- Scales seamlessly from 5 to 500+ employees
- Background check integration

**Cons:**
- Pricing not transparent — requires a sales call
- Interface feels older than Gusto or Rippling
- Can be oversold on features you won't use

## 4. Wave Payroll — Best Free-Adjacent Option

**Pricing:** $20/month + $6/employee (tax service states) / $6/employee only (self-service states)

Wave offers the lowest-cost full payroll option for US small businesses. In states where Wave handles tax filings automatically, you pay $20/month plus $6 per employee — significantly cheaper than Gusto or QuickBooks. In other states, it's self-serve (you file taxes yourself) at just $6/employee.

**Best for:** Solo operators, very small teams (1–5 employees), budget-conscious US businesses

**Pros:**
- Cheapest full-service payroll in the US market
- Free accounting software included (Wave is primarily an accounting platform)
- Direct deposit, W-2s, and 1099s included
- Simple, clean interface

**Cons:**
- Full-service tax filing only available in select US states
- Fewer integrations than Gusto or QuickBooks
- Less powerful HR features
- Customer support limited to chat/email

## 5. Rippling — Best for Tech-Forward US Businesses

**Pricing:** $8/employee/month base + modules (payroll ~$8/employee additional)

Rippling is the most ambitious HR and payroll platform in this list. It combines payroll, HR, IT management, and benefits into one unified system. When you hire someone, Rippling can automatically set up their payroll, provision their laptop, create their Google Workspace account, and enroll them in benefits — all from one workflow.

**Best for:** Tech companies, SaaS businesses, US businesses with distributed remote teams

**Pros:**
- Most automated onboarding/offboarding of any platform
- IT management built in (device management, app access)
- International payroll available (if you hire outside the US)
- Best-in-class API and integrations ecosystem

**Cons:**
- Modular pricing adds up quickly
- Complex to set up — not ideal for non-technical owners
- More than most small businesses need

## IRS Compliance: What Every US Business Must Know

The IRS charges automatic penalties for late or incorrect payroll tax deposits. These start at **2% of the unpaid amount** and escalate to **15%** for deposits more than 10 days late. The good news: every platform on this list handles deposits automatically, so you're protected as long as you run payroll on time.

You also need to file:
- **Form 941** quarterly (payroll taxes)
- **Form 940** annually (federal unemployment tax)
- **W-2s** to all employees by January 31 each year
- **1099-NECs** to all contractors paid $600+ by January 31

All five platforms above handle these automatically.
