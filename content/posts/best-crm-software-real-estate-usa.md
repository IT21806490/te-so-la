Real estate is a relationship business — and in a competitive US market, the agents who follow up fastest and most consistently win. The right CRM makes that effortless. Here's the definitive comparison of real estate CRMs for US agents in 2026.

## What to Look for in a Real Estate CRM

Not every CRM is built for real estate. The best ones include:

- **Lead capture** from Zillow, Realtor.com, and your website
- **Automated follow-up** sequences via email and SMS
- **Pipeline management** to track deals from lead to close
- **MLS integration** to attach listings to contacts
- **Mobile app** for agents who are always on the go

---

## 1. Follow Up Boss — Best Overall for US Agents

**Pricing:** $69/month (Solo) / $499/month (Team) / $1,000/month (Platform)

Follow Up Boss is the gold standard for US real estate teams. It aggregates leads from every source — Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook Ads, IDX websites — into one inbox and automatically assigns them to agents with built-in follow-up plans.

**Best for:** Individual agents and teams doing 20+ transactions/year

**Pros:**
- Connects to 200+ lead sources
- Built-in calling, texting, and email
- Action plans run automatically — no manual triggers needed
- Excellent mobile app

**Cons:**
- Expensive for solo agents just starting out
- Learning curve for full automation setup

---

## 2. LionDesk — Best Budget CRM for Solo Agents

**Pricing:** $39/month (Starter) / $49/month (Pro)

LionDesk packs serious power into an affordable package. Video texting, AI-powered lead follow-up (Gabby the AI assistant), drip campaigns, and a built-in dialer make it the best value CRM for US agents on a budget.

**Best for:** Solo agents, part-time agents, new agents

**Pros:**
- Video texting stands out in crowded inboxes
- AI assistant (Gabby) handles initial lead responses 24/7
- Very affordable at $39/month
- Simple, intuitive interface

**Cons:**
- Less powerful reporting than Follow Up Boss
- Some integrations require higher tier

---

## 3. kvCORE — Best for Brokerages

**Pricing:** Contact for quote (typically $500–$2,000+/month for teams)

kvCORE is built for brokerages and large teams. It includes a full IDX website, lead generation tools, a smart CRM, and a marketplace for third-party integrations. Many of the top US real estate brokerages run on kvCORE.

**Best for:** Brokerages, mega-teams, franchise offices

**Pros:**
- All-in-one: website + CRM + lead gen + marketing
- Behavioral lead scoring identifies hot leads automatically
- Custom branded for your brokerage

**Cons:**
- Overkill for individual agents
- Pricing not transparent — requires a sales call

---

## 4. HubSpot CRM — Best Free Option

**Pricing:** Free (core CRM) / $15/month (Starter) / $800/month (Professional)

HubSpot's free CRM is surprisingly capable for real estate agents who want to get organized without spending money. The free plan includes contact management, deal pipelines, email integration, and basic reporting.

**Best for:** New agents, budget-conscious solo agents, agents with tech backgrounds

**Pros:**
- Genuinely free with no time limit
- Best-in-class email tracking
- Huge ecosystem of integrations
- Excellent mobile app

**Cons:**
- Not built specifically for real estate — needs customization
- Real estate-specific automations require paid plans
- Can get expensive quickly as you add features

---

## 5. Wise Agent — Best for Teams Wanting Simplicity

**Pricing:** $49/month flat (unlimited users)

Wise Agent's flat-fee pricing makes it exceptional value for growing teams. Unlimited users for $49/month means you can add every agent, assistant, and transaction coordinator without worrying about per-seat costs.

**Best for:** Growing teams, small brokerages, cost-conscious teams

**Pros:**
- Flat fee = great value as team grows
- Built-in transaction management
- Landing page builder included
- Good Zillow and Realtor.com integration

**Cons:**
- UI feels slightly dated compared to competitors
- Reporting could be stronger
