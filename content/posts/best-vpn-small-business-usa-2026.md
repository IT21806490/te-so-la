Remote work is now standard for millions of US small businesses — and with it comes serious cybersecurity risk. A VPN (Virtual Private Network) encrypts your team's internet traffic, hides your business data from hackers on public Wi-Fi, and keeps your company network secure no matter where your employees work.

But not all VPNs are equal. Some are built for consumers, others for enterprises with IT departments. This guide focuses on VPNs that work specifically for US small businesses — affordable, easy to manage, and secure enough to protect real business data.

---

## Why US Small Businesses Need a VPN

The FBI's 2025 Internet Crime Report found that small businesses are now the most targeted group for cybercrime in the United States — not large enterprises. Reasons include:

- Employees using home or coffee shop Wi-Fi to access company systems
- No dedicated IT security team to monitor threats
- Cloud tools (QuickBooks, Salesforce, Slack) accessed over unencrypted connections
- Remote workers logging into company servers without protection

A business VPN solves all of these. It costs less than $10 per user per month and can prevent breaches that cost US small businesses an average of $108,000 to recover from.

## 1. NordLayer — Best Overall for US Small Businesses

**Pricing:** $8/user/month (Basic) / $11/user/month (Advanced) / Custom (Enterprise)

NordLayer is built by the same team behind NordVPN, but designed specifically for business use. It gives you centralized control over all users, dedicated IP addresses (important for accessing US-restricted tools), and integrates with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 — the two platforms most US small businesses already use.

**Best for:** Teams of 5–50 employees using cloud-based tools

**Pros:**
- Centralized admin dashboard — add/remove users in seconds
- Dedicated US IP addresses available
- Works seamlessly with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365
- Auto-connect on untrusted Wi-Fi networks
- SOC 2 certified (important for healthcare, finance, legal businesses)

**Cons:**
- Slightly more expensive than consumer VPNs
- Advanced features require higher-tier plan

**Verdict:** The best all-around choice for US small businesses that need security without hiring an IT team.

## 2. Perimeter 81 — Best for Growing Teams

**Pricing:** $8/user/month (Essential) / $12/user/month (Premium) / Custom (Enterprise)
*Minimum 5 users*

Perimeter 81 (now part of Check Point) is a cloud-based network security platform that goes beyond a simple VPN. It offers zero-trust network access — meaning employees only get access to the specific tools they need, not your entire network.

**Best for:** US businesses with 10–100 employees, especially those in regulated industries

**Pros:**
- Zero-trust architecture is the gold standard for business security
- Excellent for businesses with compliance requirements (HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI-DSS)
- Network segmentation keeps departments isolated
- Works with Okta, Azure AD, and Google SSO

**Cons:**
- Minimum 5-user requirement
- More complex than a basic VPN — needs some setup time
- More expensive than consumer options

**Pricing:** Contact for business pricing (typically $6–9/user/month)

ExpressVPN is consistently rated the fastest VPN in independent speed tests. For US businesses where employees need to access video calls, large file transfers, or US-based streaming services without slowdown, ExpressVPN delivers without compromise.

**Pros:**
- Fastest speeds of any VPN tested — minimal impact on performance

- Works on every device: Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, Linux
- 24/7 live chat support

## 4. Cisco AnyConnect — Best for IT-Managed Businesses

**Pricing:** Included with Cisco security subscriptions (varies)

If your US business already uses Cisco networking equipment or has an IT manager, Cisco AnyConnect is the enterprise-grade VPN used by more Fortune 500 companies than any other. It's complex to set up but rock-solid once running.

**Best for:** US businesses with 50+ employees or a dedicated IT manager

**Pros:**
- The most trusted name in enterprise network security
- Integrates with Cisco's full security ecosystem
- Extremely granular access controls
- Multi-factor authentication built in

**Cons:**
- Requires technical setup — not DIY-friendly
- More expensive than cloud-based alternatives
- Overkill for businesses under 20 people

## 5. Mullvad — Best for Privacy-First Businesses

Mullvad is the most privacy-focused VPN available — it doesn't require an email address to sign up, accepts cash payments, and keeps zero logs of your activity. For US businesses handling sensitive client data (law firms, healthcare, financial advisors), Mullvad's privacy guarantees are unmatched.

**Best for:** Solo practitioners, law firms, financial advisors, medical practices

**Pros:**
- No logs — verified by independent auditors
- Flat $5/device pricing — cheapest option for small teams
- No account needed — anonymous by design
- Open source and independently audited

**Cons:**
- No centralized business dashboard
- No SSO or identity provider integration
- Less polished apps than NordLayer or ExpressVPN

## What to Look for When Choosing a Business VPN

**1. Centralized management** — You need to be able to add and remove employee access instantly. If an employee leaves, you should revoke their VPN access in one click.

**2. US-based servers** — Your team needs fast connections to US servers for accessing US-based business tools. Make sure your VPN has servers across multiple US states.

**3. Compatibility** — Your team uses Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. Your VPN should work on all of them without separate subscriptions.

**4. No-log policy** — A verified no-log policy means the VPN provider cannot hand over your business activity to third parties even if legally compelled.

**5. Support** — When something breaks, you need help fast. Choose a VPN with 24/7 support, not just a help center.
