Late payments are the number one cash flow problem for US freelancers and independent contractors. The average US freelancer waits 29 days beyond their payment terms to get paid — and many invoices go unpaid entirely. The right invoicing software sends professional invoices, follows up automatically, and gets you paid faster with online payment options that clients actually use.

Here are the best invoicing tools for US freelancers and contractors in 2026.

---

## What Good Invoicing Software Does

Basic invoicing tools let you create and send invoices. Great invoicing tools do much more:

- **Online payments** — Clients pay by credit card or ACH bank transfer directly from the invoice
- **Automatic reminders** — Follow up on unpaid invoices without awkward emails
- **Recurring invoices** — Auto-bill retainer clients every month
- **Time tracking** — Convert tracked hours directly into invoice line items
- **Expense tracking** — Add billable expenses to invoices automatically
- **Tax preparation** — Generate income reports for your Schedule C

## How to Get Paid Faster as a US Freelancer

**Require deposits.** Ask for 25–50% upfront before starting work. This is standard in the US and protects you from non-payment.

**Net 14 instead of Net 30.** Change your payment terms from 30 days to 14 days. Most clients pay by the deadline — a shorter deadline means faster payment.

**Enable online payments.** Invoices with online payment options get paid 2x faster than those requiring a check. Every tool above supports this.

**Send invoices immediately.** Invoice the day you complete work, not at the end of the month. The sooner you invoice, the sooner you get paid.

**Charge late fees.** Add a 1.5% monthly late fee to your invoices. Most clients will pay on time to avoid it.
