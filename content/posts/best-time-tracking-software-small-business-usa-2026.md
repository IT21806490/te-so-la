US small businesses lose an average of $50,000 per year to inaccurate time tracking — through missed billable hours, buddy punching, and payroll errors. The right time tracking software solves all three with automated timers, GPS verification, and direct payroll integration.

Here are the best time tracking tools for US small businesses in 2026.

---

## Why Time Tracking Matters for US Businesses

The US Department of Labor requires employers to maintain accurate records of hours worked for non-exempt employees under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Inaccurate records can result in back pay claims, audits, and penalties. Beyond compliance, accurate time tracking helps you:

- Bill clients accurately for every minute of work performed
- Identify which projects are profitable and which drain resources
- Ensure payroll matches actual hours worked
- Manage remote team productivity without micromanaging

## 1. Toggl Track — Best for Freelancers and Remote Teams

**Pricing:** Free (unlimited users, basic) / $9/user/month (Starter) / $18/user/month (Premium) / Custom (Enterprise)

Toggl Track is the most popular time tracking tool among US freelancers and remote teams. Its one-click timer works in the browser, desktop app, and mobile — and an idle detection feature prompts you when you've stopped working and forgot to pause your timer. The free plan supports unlimited users, making it exceptional value for small teams.

**Best for:** US freelancers, consultants, remote teams, agencies billing by the hour

**Pros:**
- Free plan supports unlimited users — best free tier in category
- One-click timers work across browser extension, desktop, and mobile
- Idle detection — reminds you when you stop working and forgot to stop the timer
- Pomodoro timer mode for focused work sessions
- Detailed reports show billable vs non-billable hours by project and client
- Integrates with 100+ tools including Asana, Jira, and GitHub
- Calendar integration shows time blocks from Google Calendar or Outlook

**Cons:**
- No GPS tracking — not suitable for field workers
- No built-in invoicing on free plan
- Scheduling features require paid plan

## 2. Clockify — Best Completely Free Time Tracking

**Pricing:** Free (unlimited users and projects) / $4.99/user/month (Basic) / $6.99/user/month (Standard) / $11.99/user/month (Pro)

Clockify offers the most generous free plan in the time tracking category — unlimited users, unlimited projects, unlimited time entries, and basic reporting, all at no cost. For US small businesses that need simple time tracking without a budget, Clockify is genuinely free without meaningful limitations.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious US teams, businesses new to time tracking, nonprofits

**Pros:**
- Truly free for unlimited users and unlimited projects — no catch
- Timesheet approval workflow on paid plans
- Scheduling and capacity planning included
- Kiosk mode — employees clock in on a shared tablet or computer
- GPS tracking for field employees on paid plans
- Integrates with QuickBooks, Gusto, and major US payroll tools
- Available on web, desktop, and mobile

**Cons:**
- Free plan lacks invoicing, GPS, and scheduling features
- Interface less polished than Toggl or Harvest
- Mobile app reliability inconsistent

## 3. Harvest — Best for Client Billing and Invoicing

**Pricing:** Free (1 user, 2 projects) / $12/user/month (Pro, unlimited)

Harvest combines time tracking with invoicing — when a project is complete, Harvest converts tracked hours directly into a professional invoice that clients can pay online. For US service businesses and agencies that bill clients by the hour, this eliminates the manual process of calculating billable hours and creating invoices separately.

**Best for:** US agencies, consultants, professional services firms billing clients hourly

**Pros:**
- Direct time-to-invoice conversion — no manual calculation
- Online payments via Stripe and PayPal directly from invoices
- Budget tracking — alerts when a project approaches its hour or dollar limit
- Detailed reporting on team utilization and project profitability
- Integrates with Asana, Basecamp, Jira, and QuickBooks
- Forecasting tool (Harvest Forecast) for capacity planning

**Cons:**
- More expensive per user than Toggl or Clockify
- No GPS tracking for field workers
- Free plan limited to 1 user and 2 projects

## 4. QuickBooks Time — Best Payroll Integration

**Pricing:** $20/month + $8/user/month (Premium) / $40/month + $10/user/month (Elite)

QuickBooks Time (formerly TSheets) is purpose-built for US businesses that use QuickBooks for payroll. Employees clock in on mobile or desktop, hours sync automatically to QuickBooks Payroll, and managers approve timesheets with one click before payroll runs. For businesses already using QuickBooks, this eliminates manual timesheet entry entirely.

**Best for:** US businesses using QuickBooks Payroll, businesses with field or mobile workers

**Pros:**
- Direct QuickBooks Payroll sync — no manual data entry
- GPS tracking and geofencing — verify employees are on-site when they clock in
- Who's Working window shows where every employee is in real time
- Mileage tracking built in
- DCAA compliant for US government contractors
- Crew scheduling and job costing features
- Photo capture at clock-in for facial verification

**Cons:**
- Requires QuickBooks to get full value
- More expensive than standalone time trackers
- Interface less modern than Toggl or Clockify

## US Labor Law and Time Tracking Compliance

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, US employers must maintain time records for at least two years. These records must include:

- Employee's full name and Social Security number
- Hours worked each day and total hours each workweek
- Regular hourly pay rate
- Total straight-time earnings
- Total overtime earnings for the workweek

All five platforms above maintain these records automatically. Ensure your chosen platform generates reports that meet these requirements before your next payroll audit.
